# FlapBoard 1.0.0

The first FlapBoard screen saver for Mac — free for personal use.

- Display the time or one custom text of up to 60 characters.
- Enjoy a Mechanical split-flap display with staggered animation and optional sound.
- Adjust settings directly from macOS without a companion app.
- Use a fixed layout on your main display.
- Enable subtle display shifting; a full flap cycle runs every four minutes.
- Use English or German settings, selected from your preferred system language.
- Run offline, without an account.

Requires macOS 14 or later; includes Apple silicon and Intel binaries. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Display shifting and flap cycles are not a guarantee against OLED burn-in and do not replace the display manufacturer's care procedures.

The extended app and Pro features are still in preparation and are not part of this download.
